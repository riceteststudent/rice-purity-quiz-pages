What Does 70 Mean on the Rice Purity Test? A Complete Score Guide

You finished the hundred questions, the page flashed your result, and now you are staring at a single number: 70. Naturally you want to know: what does 70 mean on rice purity test scoring? Let us decode it properly — what the number measures, where it sits on the scale, and what it says (and does not say) about you.

How the 0–100 Scale Works

Everyone begins at 100. Each “yes” answer to one of the hundred questions subtracts a single point, so a score of 70 means you answered “yes” to thirty items. The questions span dating, relationships, parties, travel, and mild mischief. Thirty yes answers is a meaningful chunk of life experience — roughly a third of everything the quiz asks about — while seventy untouched boxes show there is still plenty you have not done.

Where 70 Falls on the Score Bands

There is no official score key, but test-takers have developed rough bands over the years:

Score rangeGeneral read
100–90Very high “purity”; few life experiences checked off
89–77Above average; typical for younger students
76–60Upper-middle; a balanced mix of experiences
59–45Middle of the road; average college life
44–30Below average; adventurous streak
29–0Very low; checked off most of the list

A 70 lands squarely in the upper-middle band. It is the score of someone who has dated, partied, traveled, or bent a few rules — but who still has most of the wilder boxes unchecked. In friend-group comparisons, 70 often reads as “experienced but sensible,” the kind of score that draws nods rather than gasps.

What a 70 Says About You

Thirty checked boxes usually include the classic milestones: holding hands, a first kiss, going on dates, attending parties, maybe a road trip or a curfew broken here and there. What it typically does not include are the extremes at the far end of the list. In other words, a 70 suggests you have lived enough to have stories, while keeping most of your boundaries intact — a balance many people are quietly happy with.

Why You Should Not Overthink It

Here is the important part: the number is a conversation starter, not a verdict. Two people can both score 70 with completely different sets of checked boxes. Your upbringing, values, and opportunities shape your answers far more than any personality trait does. Comparing scores with friends is fun, but letting a quiz number define you would be like letting a horoscope write your résumé.

So what does 70 mean on the rice purity test? It means you are thirty experiences into a hundred-question adventure — seasoned enough to relate, fresh enough to wonder what comes next.
